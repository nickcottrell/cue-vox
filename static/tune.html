Delivery · global, on top of the register
Break length · pause1.0x
Pause length: stretches or compresses every silence. In voice design a pause is a "beat", longer beats let a line land and read as weight.
Volume pressure · projection1.0
How much harder the voice pushes as the register climbs. This is "intensity" or "projection", the sense of leaning in.
Question rise · terminal rise0.8
How much the last syllable tilts UP: the amount it sounds like a question. High = uptalk (rising boundary tone); zero = flat, declarative, certain.
Steering
Steers Regenerate on the Tuner tab. Leave blank to just refine the existing markup.
Earn-curve · how loud is earned
The auto-tone stand-in: how the voice earns its way up to a louder register from the energy in the room. (A model will drive register directly later.)
Gamma · earn steepness2.2
How hard it is to get loud. Higher = whisper is the default and drama is expensive; lower = it jumps to loud easily.
Cap · energy budget9.0
How much built-up energy it takes to reach the top register. Higher = the peak is rare and hard-won.
Decay · cool-down0.60
How fast the energy settles between turns. Low = calms down quickly; high = stays hot longer (momentum).
Loudness floor · what counts as trying0.07
The mic level that registers as energy. Talking below this reads as calm; above it spends into the pool.
Envelope · per-register bounds
The card marked ● shaping is the register picked in the Register dropdown up top. Pick a register there, or click any card here, to choose which one you shape. They are one selection.
Each register is a timbre (how the voice sounds) plus a prosody envelope (how it reads the same markup). The envelope is a range: min = the gentlest that dimension gets in this register, max = the boldest. A wider range = a more expressive register.
Timbre
• slot: which blended voice character (0 breathy → 4 dramatic). Only bites on the neural-blend voice.
• speed: baseline talking pace (industry: rate). 1.0 is natural.
• bright: crisp vs soft (industry: brightness / presence, a high-frequency lift). Up = present and forward, down = muffled and intimate.
• gain: overall volume (industry: level).
Prosody envelope (min → max)
• break: pause length between phrases (industry: pause / dwell). Bigger = more room, weight, gravity.
• emphasis: how hard a stressed word gets punched (industry: emphasis / prominence, louder + higher + longer on that word).
• rate: how far a slow/fast tag can push the pace (industry: rate / tempo).
• lift: how much a "?" tilts the last syllable up (industry: terminal rise / boundary tone), the amount it sounds like a question.

Register voices · the neural blend per register
Set the neural voice blend for each register (chill / mid / peak). Each is a weighted mix of the base Kokoro voices, so a register can literally be a different-sounding voice. Registers themselves are added/removed on the Envelope tab; here you set what each one sounds like.
The pipeline: edit a register's mix → Save & rebuild (regenerates the blend and hot-swaps the live engine) → go to the Tuner, pick that register, and Play to hear it.
Registers
Save writes maestro/config/voice/blend.json; rebuild regenerates register-voices.bin and hot-swaps the running engine, so the next Play uses the new blend. A weight sum near 1.0 is ideal (it normalizes on rebuild).
